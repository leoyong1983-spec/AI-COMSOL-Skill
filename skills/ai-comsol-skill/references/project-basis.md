# Public research scope

This package is a reusable ethylene-glycol electrolysis research skill. Public examples are illustrative configurations, not a controlled design for a particular laboratory or industrial project.

The baseline targets alkaline EG oxidation to glycolate with competing formate/oxalate branches and paired hydrogen evolution. A separate optional 0D template includes furfural reduction. Actual catalyst, electrode area, geometry, membrane, product specification and experimental calibration must be supplied for a specific design.

Bibliographic and parameter sources are recorded in `parameter-sources.json` and `catalyst-reactor-data-v2.json`. These distinguish measured quantities, adjacent evidence, derived scenarios and assumptions. No private project source documents are included or required.

All examples preserve the distinction between numerical verification of equations and validation against experimental data. The open-source license does not provide a simulator license or engineering approval.
